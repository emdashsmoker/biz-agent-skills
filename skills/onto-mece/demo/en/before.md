**Tasks for Growing the Hyperscale AI Industry**

**1. Building AI Infrastructure**
- 1.1 Securing computing resources such as GPUs (including shared access for startups and universities)
- 1.2 Building data centers and a stable power supply
- 1.3 Building training data (including Korean-language datasets)

**2. Securing Core AI Technologies**
- 2.1 Developing Korean-language hyperscale models
- 2.2 Developing AI chips
- 2.3 Developing reliability verification technology

**3. Developing AI Talent**
- 3.1 Top researchers: training master's and PhD talent and recruiting researchers from abroad
- 3.2 Industry practitioners: retraining engineers and workers in companies
- 3.3 General public: AI literacy education for all citizens

**4. Spreading AI Across Industry and the Public Sector**
- 4.1 Private sector, specific fields: applying AI in specialized fields such as law and medicine
- 4.2 Private sector, all fields: supporting AI adoption by SMEs
- 4.3 Public sector: applying AI to public services

**5. Improving Laws and Institutions**
- 5.1 New law: enacting the AI Basic Act
- 5.2 Existing law: revising the copyright system for AI training data and outputs
- 5.3 Existing law: revising personal data rules to protect data while allowing its safe use for AI

**6. Expanding Global Reach**
- 6.1 Supporting the overseas expansion of AI companies and services
- 6.2 Cooperating internationally on AI rules and standards
