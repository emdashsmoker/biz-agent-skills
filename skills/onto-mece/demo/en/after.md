# Tasks for Growing the Hyperscale AI Industry

## I. Supply (what producers need to build hyperscale AI)

### 1. Infrastructure (shared compute and data resources that developers draw on)
- Securing computing resources such as GPUs
- Building training data (which data can be used depends on the rules in III-1)

### 2. Technology (research and development)
- Developing Korean-language models
- Developing AI chips
- Developing reliability verification technology (the means of checking AI against the trust requirements of the AI Basic Act in III-1)

### 3. Talent (people who build and run AI)
- Training advanced talent at master's and PhD level
- [New] Training working-level engineers who build and run services on hyperscale models

## II. Demand (who adopts what Part I produces, by adopter)

### 1. Government (administrative services run by public agencies)
- Applying AI to public services

### 2. Industry (businesses and professions)
- (Field level) Applying AI in specialized fields such as law and medicine
- (Firm level) Supporting SMEs in adopting AI

### 3. Citizens
- AI literacy education for all citizens

### 4. Overseas Markets
- Supporting overseas expansion

## III. Institutions (rules that Parts I and II both operate under)

### 1. Domestic Rules
- Enacting the AI Basic Act
- Revising copyright rules for AI (use of works in training, rights in AI-generated outputs)
- Strengthening personal data protection

### 2. International Rules
- Cooperating internationally on AI norms and standards
