# Content
## Survey overview
- (Purpose) Identify how staff use generative AI at work and what they need
- (Intended use) Baseline input for reviewing an in-house hyperscale AI pilot
- (Population) All 412 staff
- (Period) September 8-19, 2026 (2 weeks)
- (Method) Anonymous online survey on the company survey system
- (Questions) 14 multiple-choice and 2 open-ended questions
- (Responses) 287 responses (response rate 69.7%)
- (Definition of user) Staff who used a generative AI service for work at least once in the past 6 months
- (Respondents by question) Some questions limited to certain respondents
    + (Users only) Frequency, tasks and time saved
    + (Non-users only) Reasons for not using it

## Use by department
- (Overall) 157 of 287 respondents (54.7%) have used it
- (Office departments) Use rates of 56.4%-100.0%
- (Production team) Use rate of 22.1%, the lowest of all departments
- (Presumed cause for the Production team) Little time at work PCs due to a large share of shop-floor work (Planning Office estimate)

| Department | Respondents | Users | Use rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Quality team | 48 | 31 | 64.6% |
| Design team | 62 | 45 | 72.6% |
| Production team | 86 | 19 | 22.1% |
| Sales team | 34 | 24 | 70.6% |
| Business Support team | 39 | 22 | 56.4% |
| IT team | 5 | 5 | 100.0% |
| Planning Office | 13 | 11 | 84.6% |
| Total | 287 | 157 | 54.7% |

## Frequency of use
- (Twice a week or more) 81 of 157 users (51.6%)
    + (Daily) 23 (14.6%)
    + (2-3 times a week) 58 (36.9%)
- (Once a week or less) 76 (48.4%)
- (Adoption level) Assessed as not yet an everyday work tool

## Tasks
- (Response type) Users, multiple answers
- (Drafting documents) 98 (62.4%)
- (Searching and summarizing material) 87 (55.4%)
- (Translation) 52 (33.1%)
- (Generating ideas) 41 (26.1%)
- (Writing code) 14 (8.9%)
- (Code writers) Limited to the IT team and some Design team staff

## Compliance with the security rules
- (Current information security rules) Entering work documents into external generative AI services is prohibited
- (Aware of the rule) 201 of 287 respondents (70.0%)
- (Unaware of the rule) 86 (30.0%)
- (External input) 36 of 157 users (22.9%) have entered work documents
- (Confidential document input) 4 of the 36 said they entered confidential documents
- (Reasons for not using it) Answered by the 130 non-users
    + (Not allowed under the security rules) 58 (44.6%)
    + (Not needed for my work) 39 (30.0%)
    + (Do not know how to use it) 33 (25.4%)
- (Effect of the rules) Holding back use confirmed
- (Limit of the rules) Some users do not comply

## Perceived time saved
- (1 hour or more a week) 108 of 157 users (68.8%)
- (3 hours or more a week) 37 (23.6%)
- (Responses by range) Distribution of time saved per week
    + (Under 1 hour) 49 (31.2%)
    + (1-3 hours) 71 (45.2%)
    + (3-5 hours) 28 (17.8%)
    + (5 hours or more) 9 (5.7%)
- (Measurement limit) Respondents' own estimates, which may differ from measured values

## Concerns
- (Response type) All respondents, multiple answers
- (Answer accuracy) 168 (58.5%)
- (Information leaks) 142 (49.5%)
- (Learning to use it) 61 (21.3%)
- (Effects on performance reviews) 37 (12.9%), a low level

## Support requested from the company
- (Response type) All respondents, multiple answers
- (An in-house service) 176 (61.3%)
- (Training) 149 (51.9%)
- (Usage guidelines) 133 (46.3%)

## Open-ended responses
- (Quality team) Unable to use it to search past defect reports because uploads to external services are restricted
- (Design team) Wants to use it to organize design change history and draft change reports
- (Production team) Wants to look up work standards on a phone right on the shop floor
- (Business Support team) Cautious about use because there is no standard for the allowed scope

## Planning Office implications
- (Demand) Demand for use at work confirmed by a use rate of 54.7% and 61.3% asking for an in-house service
- (Security) Cases of work documents entered into external services, including confidential documents, confirmed (36 people)
- (Guidelines) 30.0% of staff unaware of the rules, so guidance on the scope of use is lacking
- (Impact) Actual time savings need to be measured at the pilot stage

## Next steps
- (October 2026) Draft generative AI usage guidelines, then announce them to all staff (Planning Office and Information Security)
- (October 2026) Follow up on cases of confidential documents being entered (Information Security)
- (November 2026) Report the in-house hyperscale AI pilot plan (draft) to executives (Planning Office)
- (December 2026) Set training schedules on generative AI use by department (Planning Office and HR)

# Implications
- (Demand held back by security) The 58 non-users (44.6%) who did not use it because of the security rules and the Quality team's answer show use demand left unmet by external service restrictions
- (Confidential document input) The results of the October follow-up and the scope of action need further checking
- (Impact measurement) Whether the next steps reflect the actual measurement of time saved called for in the Planning Office implications needs checking
- (Department needs) Whether the pilot plan (draft) reflects the Quality team's defect report search and the Production team's phone lookup of work standards needs checking
- (Survey coverage) With a response rate of 69.7%, the use of staff who did not respond is not reflected
