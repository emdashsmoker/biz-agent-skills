The project models the equipment, process and quality domains as an OWL 2 DL ontology with reference to the ISA-95 hierarchy model, and builds a knowledge graph by loading MES and ERP data into an RDF triplestore through R2RML mappings. At the equipment level, OPC UA information models and AAS submodels provide semantic interoperability. A reasoner derives relations that are not stated explicitly from class hierarchies and property chains, and SHACL shapes validate data integrity. The AI layer follows a neuro-symbolic design that combines knowledge graph embeddings with a GNN-based anomaly detection model, and SPARQL queries trace each detected anomaly back to root-cause candidates. For operator questions, an LLM navigates the knowledge graph through GraphRAG to generate explainable answers, and ontology schema changes are governed by versioning and regression tests based on competency questions (CQs).
