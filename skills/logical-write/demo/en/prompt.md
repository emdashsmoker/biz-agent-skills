Write a report titled "Adopting Hyperscale AI In-House" for an executive meeting at a mid-sized manufacturer. Base it on the source pack and the internal notes below.

- The readers are the CEO and division heads, and none of them are AI specialists
- They will use the report to decide whether to run a pilot in the first half of next year
- Keep it to about two A4 pages

## Source pack

1. According to the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI (HAI) "AI Index Report 2025", the inference cost of a system performing at the level of GPT-3.5 dropped more than 280-fold between November 2022 and October 2024.
2. According to the same report, the share of organizations that reported using AI rose from 55% in 2023 to 78% in 2024.
3. According to the same report, the performance gap between open-weight and closed models on some benchmarks narrowed from 8% to 1.7% in one year.
4. In April 2023, the Korean Ministry of Science and ICT announced its plan to strengthen hyperscale AI competitiveness. Starting with KRW 390.1 billion in 2023, the plan builds 200 types of training data and 130 Korean-language corpora by 2027, expands the computing resources offered to SMEs, startups, universities and research institutes from teraflop to petaflop scale, and runs five flagship projects in law, medicine, counseling, culture and the arts, and academic research. It also includes guidelines on public-sector use for civil servants.

## Internal notes (from last week's meeting)

- Company security rules do not allow work documents to be entered into external generative AI services
- The quality and design teams say that searching past documents and drafting reports take a lot of their time
- The IT team has five people and no one dedicated to AI
