# Adopting Hyperscale AI In-House

## Pilot on company servers recommended for the first half of next year
- Open-weight hyperscale artificial intelligence (AI) model on company servers as the pilot setup
    + Work documents kept inside the company network under current security rules
    + Open-weight models now close to models kept private by their developers on public performance tests

> (Hyperscale AI) Computer program trained on large volumes of text to answer questions and draft documents in everyday language

> (Open-weight model) Hyperscale AI released by its developer as files for installation on a company's own servers

- Document search and report drafting in the quality and design teams as the pilot tasks due to a reported time burden
- Approval of the pilot scope and schedule requested
    + Pilot budget for approval in January after quotes from outside implementation partners
    + Expansion beyond the two teams decided in June on measured results
- Performance on company documents and the running effort for the information technology (IT) team checked within the pilot before any wider rollout
    + Public test results not yet confirmed on company quality and design documents
    + No dedicated AI staff in the five-person IT team

## Open-weight models on company servers as a usable route within current security rules
- Entry of work documents into external generative AI services not allowed under company security rules
- Lower performance and cost barriers to AI shown in the AI Index Report 2025 of the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI (HAI)
    + Performance gap between open-weight models and models kept private by their developers down from 8% to 1.7% on some public performance tests within one year
    + Cost of producing answers with AI at the performance level of GPT-3.5 down more than 280-fold between November 2022 and October 2024

> Stanford HAI (2025), AI Index Report 2025 (https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2025-ai-index-report)

> (GPT-3.5) OpenAI model behind the first public version of ChatGPT in November 2022

## Pilot limited to document search and report drafting in the quality and design teams
- Heavy time burden of document search and report drafting reported by the quality and design teams
- Narrow scope to keep the review and support workload within current staff

| Item | Pilot content | Grounds |
|---|---|---|
| Users | Quality team and design team | Reported time burden of document search and report drafting |
| Tasks | Answers from past documents with sources shown and first drafts of reports | Text-centered work in both teams |
| Documents | Past documents of the two teams chosen by each team | Accuracy reviews by staff familiar with the documents |
| Installation | Open-weight model on a server inside the company network | Security rules on external generative AI services |

## Pilot resources limited to an outside partner, one part-time IT contact, and a company server
- Outside implementation partner in charge of the pilot system due to the absence of dedicated AI staff in the five-person IT team
    + Server access control and security checks by one IT staff member as the part-time in-house contact
    + Operating know-how passed from the partner to the IT contact during the pilot
- Pilot budget for approval in January after partner quotes
    + Company server and partner fee as the main cost items
    + Computing support of the Ministry of Science and ICT for small and medium-sized enterprises, startups, universities, and research institutes excluded from the budget plan due to unconfirmed eligibility of the company

## Expansion decided in June on measured results against thresholds set in advance
- Pilot run from January to June in preparation, use, and evaluation phases

| Phase | Period | Main work | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Preparation | January-February | Partner selection, security team review, server installation, measurement of current work time | Approved budget, security review result, current work time |
| Use | March-May | Daily use by the two teams for document search and report drafting with logging of wrong answers | Usage log and wrong-answer log |
| Evaluation | June | Comparison with current work time, review of answer accuracy, check of security logs, count of IT staff hours | Expansion recommendation |

- Expansion beyond the two teams conditional on every measure meeting its threshold
    + (Work time) Time per document search and per report draft against the current work time
    + (Answer accuracy) Share of answers citing the correct source documents in reviews by each team
    + (Security compliance) No work documents sent outside the company network in IT security logs
    + (Running effort) Monthly IT staff hours spent on the pilot system
- Threshold for each measure set by executives at the end of February after the measurement of current work time
