# Adopting Hyperscale AI In-House

## Pilot in the first half of 2027 recommended
- Pilot of hyperscale artificial intelligence (AI) on company servers recommended for the quality and design teams from January to June 2027
    + Both teams name past-document search and report drafting as heavy time burdens
    + AI models that run on company servers now score close to leading AI services on some standard performance tests
    + Work documents stay inside the company network under current security rules
    + External implementation partner covers the lack of AI staff in the five-person information technology (IT) team

> (Hyperscale AI) Software of the ChatGPT kind that answers questions and drafts documents in ordinary language after training on very large volumes of text

- Expansion to other departments decided in June 2027 by comparing the labor cost of hours saved with the annual operating cost
- Approval requested at this meeting for the pilot launch, the two participating teams, and collection of partner quotes to set the budget ceiling

## AI on company servers now realistic enough to test
- Price and performance trends favor AI on company servers
    + Cost of running an AI system at GPT-3.5 level down more than 280-fold between November 2022 and October 2024
    + Performance gap between open-weight models and models offered as a service by their developers narrowed from 8% to 1.7% on some standard performance tests within one year

> (GPT-3.5) OpenAI model behind the first public version of ChatGPT released in November 2022

> (Open-weight model) AI model whose developer publishes the trained model files for anyone to download and run on their own servers

> Stanford HAI (2025), AI Index Report 2025 (https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2025-ai-index-report)

- Fit with this company's documents and servers still unverified
    + Standard performance tests use general tasks unrelated to company documents
    + Server and setup costs for this company fall outside these figures
    + Pilot needed to measure accuracy and cost on actual quality and design documents

## Document work suited to a first pilot
- Past-document search and report drafting suited to a first pilot in the quality and design teams
    + Quality and design teams named both tasks at last week's meeting as taking much of their working time
    + Both tasks start from text already held in company documents
    + Hyperscale AI handles text search and drafting from questions in ordinary language
- Hours spent on both tasks not yet measured
    + Size of possible savings unknown without a baseline
    + Two-week time log in January 2027 needed to set the baseline for judging pilot results

## Pilot design fitted to internal constraints
- (Server location) AI model installed on servers inside the company network
    + Current security rules disallow entry of work documents into external generative AI services
    + Open-weight model chosen for its availability for installation on company servers
    + IT team security check required before document loading
- (Setup) Installation and document loading carried out by an external implementation partner
    + No staff dedicated to AI among the five members of the IT team
    + One IT staff member named pilot owner to take over operation after the pilot
- (Scope) Pilot limited to document search and report drafting in the quality and design teams
    + Narrow scope keeps document preparation within the capacity of the partner and the IT pilot owner
    + Results from two teams compared directly with each team's January baseline
- (Timing) Waiting for further AI price declines brings little saving to the pilot
    + Further price declines reduce the server and model share of pilot cost
    + Document organizing and staff training cost the same regardless of model prices
    + Server lease for the pilot period avoids fixing hardware spending at current prices

## Pilot schedule ending in an expansion decision in June 2027
- Pilot run in stages from partner selection in late 2026 to an expansion decision in June 2027

| Period | Stage | Main work | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| November-December 2026 | Partner selection | Quotes from at least two partners, budget approval, eligibility check for government support, partner contract | IT team |
| January 2027 | Preparation | Two-week time log, selection of past documents to load, server lease | Team leads and IT pilot owner |
| February-March 2027 | Setup | Server installation, document loading, IT team security check | Partner and IT pilot owner |
| April-May 2027 | Trial use | User training, daily use in both tasks, repeat time log | Team members and partner |
| June 2027 | Evaluation | Comparison of savings with operating cost and a report to the executive meeting | IT pilot owner |

- Expansion decision in June 2027 based on hours saved, output accuracy, and security record
    + (Hours saved) Labor cost of hours saved in the April-May time log compared with the annual operating cost
    + (Accuracy) Search results and report drafts rated by team leads on sampled outputs
    + (Security) Zero transfers of company documents outside the company network as a condition for expansion
- Pilot budget set from partner quotes without counting on government support
    + Ministry of Science and ICT plan of April 2023 expands computing capacity offered to small and medium-sized enterprises, startups, universities, and research institutes
    + Eligibility of a mid-sized manufacturer for this support unconfirmed
    + Manufacturing absent from the plan's flagship projects in law, medicine, counseling, culture and the arts, and academic research
