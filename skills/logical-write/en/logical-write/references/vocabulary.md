# Word list

> Translated from the Korean original. See the translation notice in SKILL.md. The words are Korean and keep their original form, followed by English glosses. English-language LLM words appear in their own section.

A list of words LLMs habitually overuse, sorted into preferred, non-preferred, and cautionary. The criteria are reader acceptance, how clearly a word marks text as LLM output, and how natural it sounds, more than whether its meaning is right or wrong. Words with the same meaning can land in different groups when their tone differs.

## Criteria

- (Preferred) Easy for readers to accept, does not mark text as LLM output, and fits formal report style naturally.
- (Non-preferred) Meets one or more of these conditions. The word is statistically overused by LLMs and marks text as LLM output. The word is empty, so the sentence keeps its meaning without it. The word is aggressive and intimidates readers (차단 "block", 배제 "exclude", 도태 "weed out", 금지 "prohibit"). The word is assertive and closes off rebuttal (절대 "absolutely", 오직 "only", 유일 "sole", 무조건 "unconditionally"). The word exaggerates scale or degree without grounds (획기적 "groundbreaking", 폭발적 "explosive", 전례 없는 "unprecedented"). The word is translationese copying English or Japanese structure. The word has a colloquial or vulgar connotation and is avoided in official documents (넣다 "put in", 빼다 "take out", 박다 "hammer in", 쑤시다 "poke").
- (Cautionary) Usable in fitting contexts, but overuse dulls its emphasis and lowers the word density of the whole text.

## Application

- When writing, choose preferred words over non-preferred ones. Use cautionary words only when the context truly needs them, at minimum frequency. Do not repeat a cautionary word twice or more in one paragraph.
- When revising, find non-preferred words and check whether a preferred word can replace them. Confirm the sentence keeps its meaning after replacement.
- This list covers vocabulary only. It does not touch sentence structure, ending style, macro structure, or length.

### Preferred

연계 (linkage), 협력 (cooperation), 전략 (strategy), 방향 (direction), 비전 (vision), 시사점 (implication), 변화 (change), 발전 (development), 융합 (convergence), 전환 (transition), 개선 (improvement), 차별화 (differentiation), 기여 (contribution), 보완 (supplement), 단계 (stage), 수요 (demand), 여건 (conditions), 현황 (current status), 실태 (actual state), 추이 (trend line), 동향 (trends), 전망 (outlook), 재원 (funding source), 인프라 (infrastructure), 거점 (hub site), 실증 (demonstration), 시범 (pilot), 확산 (spread), 이행 (implementation), 전주기 (full life cycle), 동시 (simultaneous), 필요 (need), 추가 (addition)

### Non-preferred

- (Abstract LLM nouns) 맥락 (context), 담론 (discourse), 지형 (landscape), 지평 (horizon), 함의 (implication), 층위 (layer), 층 (tier), 계위 (level), 위계 (hierarchy), 흐름 (flow). Used without a concrete target, as in "이러한 맥락에서" (in this context), "기술 지형" (technology landscape), "경쟁 지형" (competitive landscape), "새로운 지평을 열다" (open new horizons), "다양한 층위에서" (at various layers), "거대한 흐름" (great tide). 맥락 is the word LLMs overuse most
- (Paired with count announcements) 축 (axis), 종 (kind), 이중 (dual), 삼중 (triple). Overused with count announcements such as "두 축" (two axes), "세 축" (three axes), "세 종" (three kinds)
- (Unnatural nouns) 결 (grain), 틀 (frame), as in "같은 결" (same grain), "비슷한 결" (similar grain), "해당 틀" (that frame), "평가틀" (evaluation frame)
- (Overworked metaphors) 뼈대 (skeleton), 촉매 and 촉매제 (catalyst), 디딤돌 (stepping stone), 초석 (cornerstone), 교두보 (beachhead), 청사진 (blueprint), 나침반 (compass), 등대 (lighthouse), 이정표 (milestone), 돌파구 (breakthrough), as in "전략의 뼈대" (skeleton of the strategy), "혁신의 촉매" (catalyst of innovation), "발전의 초석" (cornerstone of development), "시장 교두보" (market beachhead), "미래 청사진" (blueprint for the future), "정책의 나침반" (compass of policy), "혁신의 등대" (lighthouse of innovation), "새로운 이정표" (new milestone), "돌파구 마련" (finding a breakthrough)
- (Exaggeration) 패러다임 (paradigm), 게임체인저 (game changer), 판도 (balance of power), 폭증 (explosion), 비약 (leap), 최강 (strongest), 최약 (weakest), 고아 (orphan), overused 전환점 (turning point), overused 최초 (first ever). "패러다임 전환" (paradigm shift) is the signature LLM exaggeration. "판도를 바꿀" (game-changing) and "비약적 성장" (leaping growth) are the same
- (Aggressive words) 도태 (weeding out), 선점 (preemption), 선제 (preemptive), as in "시장 선점" (market preemption), "선제적 대응" (preemptive response)
- (Assertive judgment) 직결 (direct link), 인과 (causation), 쟁점 (point of contention), 선결 (prerequisite), 준거 (standard of reference), and judgment words ending in -결
- (Authoritarian tone) 엄수 (strict observance)
- (Unneeded loanwords) 프레임 (frame), 레버리지 (leverage), 컨틴전시 (contingency), 게이트 (gate, as in "단계별 게이트" stage gates), 커버리지 (coverage), 헷지 (hedge), MVP, 통합패키지 (integrated package)
- (Direct English borrowings) 딥다이브 (deep dive, replace with 심층 분석 "in-depth analysis"), 인사이트 (insight, replace with 시사점 "implication"), 피보팅 (pivoting), 게이트키핑 (gatekeeping), 에코시스템 (ecosystem, replace with 생태계), 트랜스포메이션 (transformation, replace with 디지털 전환 "digital transition"), 풀스택 (full stack, as in "풀스택 솔루션", replace with 전주기 "full life cycle"), 매핑 (mapping, replace with 대응 "correspondence", 연결 "link", or 배치 "placement")
- (LLM favorites) 괴리 (disconnect, as in "인식과 현실의 괴리" gap between perception and reality), 묵음 (muting), 차등 (differentiated grading), 전량 (entire quantity), 뽑기 (picking) and every other form of 뽑다 (pick) such as 뽑음, 병렬 (parallel), 한정 (limitation), 병목 (bottleneck) outside real process analysis, 값 (value) outside engineering contexts, 갈래 (branch) used in place of "branch" or "path"
- (Forced noun) 온전 (wholeness), formed by cutting 한 off the adjective 온전한 (whole)

### Cautionary

Do not overuse these words.

핵심 (core), 본질 (essence), 근본 (root), 원칙 (principle), 결과 (result), 영향 (impact), 역량 (capability), 리스크 (risk), 한계 (limit), 격차 (gap), 문제 (problem), 이슈 (issue), 과제 (task), 구조 (structure), 정합 (consistency), 검증 (verification), 진단 (diagnosis), 평가 (evaluation), 판단 (judgment), 통제 (control), 관리 (management), 책임 (responsibility), 근거 (grounds), 객관 (objectivity), 주관 (subjectivity), 위험 (danger), 위협 (threat), 규제 (regulation), 준법 (legal compliance), 컴플라이언스 (compliance), 논리 (logic), 우선 (priority), 순위 (ranking), 연결 (connection), 결합 (combination), 구성 (composition), 규칙 (rule), 명제 (proposition), 유보 (reservation), 확정 (finalization), 조직 (organization), 인사 (personnel), 보상 (compensation), 소속 (affiliation), 결정 (decision), 단일 (single), 매칭 (matching), 탁월 (excellence), 훌륭 (splendid)

Use these words only under their conditions.

- (핵심 core) Only for what is truly core
- (본질 essence, 근본 root, 원칙 principle) Not to lend abstract authority
- (결론 conclusion) Non-preferred when paired with meta commentary, as in "결론적으로" (in conclusion)
- (영역 area) Only with a concrete target. Do not overuse when classifying
- (환경 environment) Meaning blurs in broad uses such as "환경 변화" (environmental change)
- (체계 system, 구축 build) Do not overuse "체계 구축" (system building), "체계 수립" (system establishment), "시스템 구축" (system construction)
- (고도화 advancement) Do not overuse, as in "시스템 고도화" (system advancement). Use for real improvement of an existing system
- (선도 leading) Non-preferred when overused as the modifier 선도적 (leading-edge)
- (거버넌스 governance) When referring to a real organizational operating structure
- (가드 guard, 가드레일 guardrail) 가드 alone is not recommended. 가드레일 carries strong security, regulatory, and institutional meaning, so judge whether the context truly needs it
- (기반 foundation) Compounds such as "기반 조성" (laying the foundation) and "기반 구축" (building the foundation) are fine. Avoid it standing alone
- (바우처 voucher) When referring to a policy instrument
- (생태계 ecosystem) When referring to a structure where several actors really interact
- (시너지 synergy) When stating the specific effect
- (로드맵 roadmap) When referring to a real schedule. Not as rhetoric for an abstract vision
- (허브 hub) When referring to a real hub function
- (플랫폼 platform) When referring to a real technical platform
- (솔루션 solution) When referring to a real product or service
- (아젠다 agenda) When referring to a real agenda
- (이니셔티브 initiative) When referring to a real program
- (벤치마크 benchmark) When referring to a real comparison standard
- (마중물 priming water) Limited use as a metaphor for initial policy investment
- (교차 cross) Do not overuse meta-style when describing methods
- (입력 input, 출력 output) Only in engineering contexts
- (언어 language) Only for real languages such as Korean, English, or language models. Not in a figurative sense

## Verbs and predicates

### Preferred

수립 (establish), 확보 (secure), 도입 (introduce), 증대 (increase), 도출 (derive), 조성 (create), 전환 (transition), 설정 (set), 확장 (expand), 부합 (fit), 개방 (open), 기여 (contribute), 연계 (link), 협력 (cooperate), 추진 (pursue), 마련 (prepare), 반영 (reflect), 적용 (apply), 운영 (operate), 활용 (use), 제고 (raise), 경감 (ease), 완화 (relieve), 촉진 (promote), 포함 (include), 추가 (add), 제외 (exclude from scope), 삭제 (delete), 차감 (deduct), 병기 (state alongside)

### Non-preferred

- (Exaggeration) overused 선도 (lead), 아우르다 (encompass, as in "전 분야를 아우르는" encompassing every field), 망라 (cover everything), 극대화 (maximize, as in "효과를 극대화" maximize the effect), 군림 (reign), overused 주도 (spearhead), 석권 (sweep), 압도 (overwhelm), 촉발 (trigger, as in "변화를 촉발" trigger change)
- (Aggressive words) 차단 (block), 배제 (exclude), 회피 (evade), 도태 (weed out), 금지 (prohibit; quotations of regulations are exempt)
- (Translationese verbs) 야기 (cause), 수반 (entail), 내포 (imply), 탈피 (break away, as in "기존 방식을 탈피" break away from the old way)
- (Assertion posing as neutral) 좌우 (determine)
- (Unused in work or daily language) 통독 (reading through), overused to mean reading the whole text
- (Colloquial or vulgar connotation) 넣다 (put in; replace with 포함 "include" or 추가 "add"), 빼다 (take out; replace with 제외 "exclude" or 삭제 "delete"), 박다 (hammer in; replace with 명시 "specify" or 고정 "fix"), 쑤시다 (poke; replace with 점검 "inspect" or 조사 "investigate"), 동시삽입 (simultaneous insertion; replace with 병기 "state alongside"). All inflected forms count, such as "넣을 것", "뺄 것", "빼고", "코드에 박아 넣다" (hard-code), "박음", "쑤심"

### Cautionary

- (혁신 innovate) Only for change that differs fundamentally from what existed
- (견인 drive) In phrases such as "성장을 견인" (drive growth), only when the causal link is clear
- (포괄 encompass) Only when the scope is stated
- (구축 build) For real new development
- (고도화 advance) For real improvement of an existing system
- (판단 judge) Non-preferred when paired with an empty obligation, as in "-라고 판단됨" (it is judged that)
- (확인 confirm) Non-preferred when paired with meta commentary, as in "확인된 바에 따르면" (according to what was confirmed)
- (강화 strengthen) Do not attach to every policy recommendation. Use with a concrete method
- (검증 verify, 진단 diagnose, 평가 evaluate) Avoid overuse
- (번역 translate, 통역 interpret) Only for real translation or interpretation between languages. Not in a figurative sense
- (삽입 insert) Fine as a document-editing function name, as in "그림 삽입" (insert figure). In narrative text, replace with 추가 (add) or 배치 (place)
- (주입 inject) Fine as a technical term, as in "SQL 주입" (SQL injection). In narrative text, replace with 반영 (reflect) or 적용 (apply)
- (투입 input) For inputs of resources such as staff or budget

## Adjectives, adverbs, modifiers

### Preferred

단계적 (phased), 점진적 (gradual), 단기 (short-term), 중장기 (mid- to long-term), 구체적 (concrete), 명확한 (clear)

### Non-preferred

- (Exaggerated modifiers) 획기적 (groundbreaking), 혁신적 (innovative), 선도적 (leading-edge), 압도적 (overwhelming), 파격적 (unconventional), 전례 없는 (unprecedented), 초유의 (first-ever), 비약적 (leaping), 폭발적 (explosive), 경이로운 (astonishing), 전방위적 (all-around)
- (Exclamatory evaluation) 탁월한 (excellent), 훌륭한 (splendid)
- (Core of contrast emphasis) 진정한 (true, as in "진정한 혁신" true innovation)
- (Translationese modifiers) 궁극적 (ultimately), 총체적 (holistic), 다층적 (multilayered), 역동적 (dynamic), 즉각적 (immediate)
- (Abstract authority) overused 근본적 (fundamental), overused 본질적 (essential)
- (Lost specificity) overused 종합적 (comprehensive), overused 포괄적 (inclusive)
- (Assertive words) 즉시 (immediately), 절대 (absolutely), 오직 (only), 유일 (sole), 무조건 (unconditionally)
- (Overused modifiers) 맞춤 and 맞춤형 (customized), overused 최우선 (top priority; when everything is top priority, the word means nothing)
- (Empty intensifiers) 매우 (very), 상당히 (quite), 굉장히 (extremely), 사실상 (virtually), 그야말로 (truly)

### Cautionary

- (특히 in particular) Overuse turns every item into "in particular" and kills its emphasis
- (결정적 decisive) Exaggerates when overused
- (핵심적 core) A variant of 핵심, so avoid overuse
- (전략적 strategic) Do not attach to every activity
- (체계적 systematic) Do not overuse, as in "체계적 접근" (systematic approach)
- (실질적 substantive) Only when concrete content follows
- (효과적 effective) Avoid overuse
- (적극적 active, 지속적 continuous) Do not attach to every policy recommendation
- (다각적 multifaceted) Only when the perspectives are named
- (범정부적 whole-of-government) For real collaboration across several ministries
- (유기적 organic) In "유기적 연계" (organic linkage), only when the specific linking structure is stated
- (확정 definite) Assertive, so use with care
- (온전한 whole) Only when wholeness has really been confirmed
- (The particles -으로 and -로) Overuse makes sentences awkward, so check their frequency

## Linking expressions

Do not use the linking expressions below. Avoid this list in flow mode as well.

- (Empty filler) 이러한 맥락에서 (in this context), 해당 맥락을 고려하면 (considering the context), 이러한 관점에서 볼 때 (from this perspective), 이러한 측면에서 (in this respect), 이러한 흐름 속에서 (amid this flow)
- (Overused conjunctions) 그렇기 때문에 (therefore), 이는 (this)
- (Translationese) 나아가 (furthermore), 더불어 (along with), 뿐만 아니라 (not only), 궁극적으로 (ultimately), 추가적으로 (additionally)
- (Meta commentary) 결론적으로 (in conclusion), 요약하자면 (to summarize), 정리하면 (to sum up), 이와 관련하여 (in this regard)
- (Invites tautology) 다시 말해 (in other words)
- (Noise) overused 즉 (that is)
- (Transition without relation) 한편 (meanwhile) and 반면 (whereas) without a real contrast, 이를 바탕으로 (based on this) without a real link, 이를 통해 (through this) with an unclear causal link

## Translationese

Expressions that copy Japanese or English sentence structure.

| Expression | Origin | Replacement |
|---|---|---|
| -에 있어서 (in regard to) | Japanese において | -에서, -시점에 |
| -인 셈이다 (amounts to) | Japanese わけだ | State it directly in noun form |
| -것은 아닐까 (might it not be) | Japanese ではないか | State the proposition directly |
| -라고 하는 (called) | Japanese という | Reorder "X라고 하는 Y" into "Y인 X" |
| -하는듯 (seems to) | Japanese ようだ | "-으로 추정" (estimated to) |
| -하지 않으면 안 된다 (must not fail to) | Japanese しなければならない | "-할 필요" (need to), "-이 요구됨" (is required) |
| -할 수밖에 없다 (cannot but) | Double negation | "-할 필요" (need to), "-이 불가피" (is unavoidable) |
| Overused -적 (的, "-ic") | 혁신적, 전략적, 구조적 three or more times in one paragraph | Rebuild in natural Korean |
| Overused -화 (化, "-ization") | 디지털화, 고도화, 가속화 twice or more in one sentence | Rebuild in natural Korean |
| -에 대하여, -을 통하여, -로 인하여 (about, through, due to) | | "-에 대해", "-을 통해", "-로 인해", or state the cause directly |
| -하는 것 (the doing of) | | Use the verb directly when the construction is unneeded |
| -의 경우 (in the case of) | English "in the case of" | Name the subject directly |
| Overused -각 (角), -축 (軸), -층, -중 | "세 각" (three angles), "두 축" (two axes), "n중 구조" (n-fold structure), "m층 체계" (m-tier system) | Name the things directly |

## Phrases and idioms

### Non-preferred

- (Evasive conclusions) 시사하는 바가 크다 (has significant implications), 시사점을 제공한다 (offers implications), 의미가 있다 (is meaningful)
- (Calling attention) 중요한 것은 (what matters is), 주목할 점은 (the notable point is), 여기서 핵심은 (the key here is), 강조할 부분은 (what I want to stress is)
- (Double-negative emphasis and ungrounded assertion) 간과할 수 없는 (cannot be overlooked), 부정할 수 없다 (cannot be denied), 분명하다 (it is clear), 자명하다 (self-evident), 두말할 나위 없다 (goes without saying)
- (Contrast emphasis) 단순한 A가 아니라 B (not merely A but B), A가 아닌 진정한 B (not A but true B), A를 넘어 B로 (beyond A to B), A에 그치지 않고 B (not stopping at A but B)
- (Count announcements) N대 전략 (N major strategies), N가지 방안 (N measures), N종 핵심 과제 (N key tasks), N대 추진 방향 (N directions)
- (Self-posed questions and dramatic phrasing) 왜 -인가? 그것은 (why is it...? because), -. 바로 그것이다 (... that is it)
- (Filler) overused "-라는 점에서" (in that), overused "-(이)라는 측면에서" (in the aspect of)
- (Abstract repackaging) -라는 단일 메커니즘 (the single mechanism called), -라는 거대한 흐름 속에서 (amid the great tide called), -이라는 과제를 안고 (carrying the task called), -이라는 본질적 가치 (the essential value called), and "-로서의" as in "수단으로서의 AI" (AI as a means)
- (Vague partial assessments) 절반은 맞다 (half right), 상당 부분 동의할 수 있다 (largely agreeable)
- (Overgeneralization) 산업 전체의 패러다임 전환을 의미 (signals a paradigm shift for the whole industry), 전 세계적으로 주목받고 있다 (drawing global attention), 글로벌 트렌드로 자리잡고 있다 (becoming a global trend), 다양한 분야에서 활발히 활용되고 있다 (actively used in various fields, with no field named)
- (Cliché openings) -의 시대가 도래하였다 (the era of ... has arrived), -의 중요성이 날로 증가하고 있다 (the importance of ... grows daily), -에 대한 관심이 높아지고 있다 (interest in ... is rising), -의 필요성이 대두되고 있다 (the need for ... is emerging)

### Cautionary

- (-을 위한 기반을 마련, lay the foundation for) Only when concrete content follows
- (시너지 효과를 창출, create synergy) Only when the specific effect is stated
- (선순환 구조를 구축, build a virtuous cycle) Only when the path of the cycle is stated
- (-와의 연계를 강화, strengthen links with) Only when the partner and method are concrete
- (지속가능한 성장, sustainable growth) Only when concrete conditions follow
- (전략적 방향성, strategic directionality) 전략적 and 방향성 are each overused, so take care when pairing them

## Checklist

- Are non-preferred nouns (맥락, 담론, 지형, 함의, 패러다임, 결, 매핑) absent?
- Are non-preferred modifiers (획기적, 혁신적, 선도적, 압도적, 진정한) and adverbs (매우, 상당히, 굉장히, 그야말로, 궁극적으로) absent?
- Are non-preferred linking expressions ("이러한 맥락에서", "결론적으로") absent?
- Are translationese and literal forms of English LLM words absent?
- Are inflected forms of colloquial or vulgar verbs (넣다, 빼다, 박다, 쑤시다) absent?
- Are contrast emphasis, self-posed questions, and count announcements absent?
- Did you write concrete grounds in place of exaggerated evaluations?
- Does no cautionary word appear twice or more in one paragraph?

## When these rules do not apply

- Original quotations
- Texts that explain AI technology itself, where a few non-preferred words such as 맥락 (context) are truly needed. Keep their frequency minimal
- Proper nouns, legal terms, and names of technical standards
- Words the user deliberately asks for
- Detect verbs such as 넣다 and 빼다 by their base form. Words with different base forms, such as 빼앗다 (seize), 빼어나다 (excel), and 빼곡하다 (densely packed), are out of scope
