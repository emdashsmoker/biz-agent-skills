# In-house hyperscale AI pilot plan (draft)

## Background
- The quality and design teams say that searching past documents and drafting reports take a lot of time
- The cost of hyperscale AI has dropped sharply, to a level a mid-sized company can adopt

## Current state
- Company security rules forbid entering work documents into external generative AI services
- IT team of 5 people, no one dedicated to AI

## Goals
- (Pilot teams) Quality and design teams
- (Impact) Cut the two teams' document work time by 50%
- (Security) Comply with company security rules

## Approach
- (Model) Connect the highest-performing external cloud hyperscale AI service by API and query company documents directly
- (Build and operations) The IT team develops and runs the integration with company systems in-house
- (Schedule) Start in January 2027, briefing for all departments in March, company-wide rollout in April

## Budget
- Service fees of KRW 120 million a year (estimated from the external service's published pricing)

## Expected benefits
- KRW 300 million a year in labor cost savings from cutting document work time by 50%
