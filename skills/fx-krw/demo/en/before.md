### Overseas hyperscale AI investment trends

Overseas companies' moves in hyperscale AI show up in three areas: revenue, investment capacity and infrastructure spending. Company A's 2024 AI revenue was $12.5 billion, nearly double the year before, which shows that AI is becoming a business that earns real revenue. Company B held $38 billion in cash and cash equivalents at the end of 2024, which gives it the financial room to expand AI investment. Company C invested €4.8 billion in AI data centers over 2025. The market outlook is also positive. Market research firm D expects the global hyperscale AI market to reach $1.3 trillion by 2030. With revenue growth, ample funds and an expanding market all in view, overseas hyperscale AI investment is likely to continue for some time.
